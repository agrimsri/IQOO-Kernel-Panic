TraceVeil includes the database unmodified (assets/geo/dbip-country-lite.mmdb) and reads it on
this phone only, to place the IP addresses in DNS answers in countries on the endpoint map.
The map labels those countries as inferred and approximate, and prints this credit whenever the
table placed them.
