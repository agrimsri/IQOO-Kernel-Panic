Legal text excerpts: sources

TraceVeil's legal lens shows short excerpts of laws, regulations and regulator guidance as
context for a finding, never as a verdict. Each excerpt is stored with the name of its source and,
where it has one, the web address it was quoted from. Quoting them here does not mean that any
publisher has endorsed TraceVeil.

law/passages.json
  European Union: GDPR (EU) 2016/679 and the ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC, from EUR-Lex
    (eur-lex.europa.eu); EDPB Guidelines 05/2020 on consent (www.edpb.europa.eu)
  United Kingdom: Information Commissioner's Office guidance, including the guidance on storage
    and access technologies (PECR) and the Age appropriate design code (ico.org.uk)
  California, United States: California Civil Code (CCPA) and the CCPA Regulations
    (Cal. Code Regs. tit. 11), as published by the California Privacy Protection Agency (cppa.ca.gov)
  United States (children): the COPPA Rule, 16 CFR Part 312, from the Electronic Code of Federal
    Regulations (www.ecfr.gov)
  Brazil: Lei n. 13.709/2018 (LGPD) and Lei n. 15.211/2025, from www.planalto.gov.br
  Singapore: Personal Data Protection Act 2012, from Singapore Statutes Online (sso.agc.gov.sg);
    PDPC Advisory Guidelines on children's personal data (www.pdpc.gov.sg)
  Global baseline: good-practice statements written by TraceVeil (not law)
