TrackerLens hostname model (ml/tracker_hostname.json)

This file holds model weights that TraceVeil trained from the lists below. It is shared under the
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported licence (CC BY-SA 3.0), not under
TraceVeil's own MIT licence.

Weights are derived from EasyPrivacy/EasyList (CC BY-SA 3.0) and Majestic Million (CC BY 3.0).

         https://easylist.to/easylist/easylist.txt (used only to remove ad hosts from the negatives)
  Licence: GPL-3.0-or-later or CC BY-SA 3.0 (or later), https://easylist.to/pages/licence.html;
           used under CC BY-SA 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

Majestic Million
  Attribution: Majestic (https://majestic.com/)
  Data: https://majestic.com/reports/majestic-million (negative labels: popular domains)
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0),
           https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Changes: the lists were reduced to registrable sites, labelled and used to train a hashed
character n-gram logistic regression; only the trained weights are in this app, not the lists.
