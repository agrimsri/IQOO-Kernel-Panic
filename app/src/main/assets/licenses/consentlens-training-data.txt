ConsentLens-1 model: notices for its training data

ConsentLens-1 (assets/consentlens/consentlens_int8.tflite) is TraceVeil's own model. Its weights
were trained on the user-interface strings below. No source string is included in this app, except
the hand-written demo phrases in consentlens_meta.json.
