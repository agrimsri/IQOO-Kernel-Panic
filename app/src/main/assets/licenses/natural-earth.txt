Natural Earth country outlines

Country outlines: Natural Earth (public domain).

Data: Natural Earth 1:110m Admin 0 - Countries, release v5.1.2,
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/110m-admin-0-countries/
from https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector (commit f1890d9f152c896d250a77557a5751a93d494776).

Natural Earth's terms of use (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) say:
"All versions of Natural Earth raster + vector map data found on this website are in the public
domain." and "No permission is needed to use Natural Earth. Crediting the authors is unnecessary."

TraceVeil projected the outlines to Mercator and keyed them by country code for the endpoint
map; the steps are listed in docs/ATTRIBUTION.md in TraceVeil's source.
