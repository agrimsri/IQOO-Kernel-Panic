Tracker catalogue and company facts: sources

TraceVeil's tracker catalogue, app-owner list, fix templates and SDK signatures
(trackers/, fixes/, sdk/) are TraceVeil's own data under its MIT licence. Company facts in the
catalogue quote short passages from the documents below; each fact in the app keeps its source
address and the date it was read (10 October 2026).

  Alphabet Inc., Form 10-K for fiscal year 2023 (sec.gov)
  Google Terms of Service (policies.google.com/terms)
  Meta Platforms, Inc., Form 10-K for fiscal year 2025 (sec.gov)
  Microsoft Corporation, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2025 (sec.gov)
  Braze, Inc., Form 10-K for fiscal year 2026 (sec.gov)
  Unity Software Inc., Form 10-K for fiscal year 2025 (sec.gov)
  AppLovin Corporation, SEC filing (sec.gov)
  Amplitude, Inc., Form 10-K for fiscal year 2025 (sec.gov)
  AppsFlyer Terms of Use (www.appsflyer.com/legal/terms-of-use/)

Website trackers (web/web_trackers.json and the catalogue's domainSource fields) cite the vendors'
own documentation pages, read on 11 October 2026, for each domain, tracking path and cookie name;
ATTRIBUTIONS.md lists them.

Company names are used only to identify the companies; TraceVeil is not affiliated with them.
